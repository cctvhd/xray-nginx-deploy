Forty hands, one pair of shoes.

Each order is cut to order and made by a single bench — start to finish — over eleven working days.

Why we still make shoes by hand

Machines stamp out shoes in forty seconds. We’d rather make them the way they were made a century ago — because a Goodyear-welted sole can be replaced, and a shoe you can resole is a shoe you keep.

Leather is cut from the whole hide, never pre-stamped. Uppers are hand-stitched with bonded nylon thread. Each sole is attached, trimmed and burnished by the same maker who signs the inside of your pair.
