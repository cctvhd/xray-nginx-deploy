Latency-Optimal Routing in Heterogeneous Wide-Area Networks: A Formal Analysis

M. Kovalenko, A. Shen, R. Krishnamurthy, L. Fontaine  —  ETH Zürich · Stanford NSEL · INRIA Rennes

Abstract

We present a formal framework for characterizing latency-optimal routing policies in heterogeneous wide-area networks where link capacities, propagation delays, and traffic demands are subject to stochastic variation. Building on a fluid-flow model augmented with bounded delay distributions, we derive closed-form bounds for end-to-end latency under both FIFO and work-conserving disciplines. Our analysis reveals a previously unexplored trade-off between path diversity and queuing stability that has practical implications for content-delivery and overlay-network design.

Keywords: WAN routing queuing theory overlay networks stochastic delay path diversity
